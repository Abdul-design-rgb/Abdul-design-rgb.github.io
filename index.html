MK-CARS Digital Transformation

MK-CARS, located in Huye District (Southern Province), is planning to modernize its legacy PHP stock and sales platform into a scalable React.js solution with real-time responsiveness and efficient resource usage.

Current System Scope

  • Stock Management
  • Orders Management
  • Car Sales Management
  • Product Advertisement
  • Product Quality Control
  • Employee Management

Current Challenges

  • Low scalability under growing business operations
  • Slow rendering due to full real-DOM updates
  • Long page loading times and delayed interactions
  • High resource consumption and maintenance overhead
  • Delayed real-time data updates for operational decisions

Proposed React.js Upgrade

  • Component-based UI for modular development
  • Virtual DOM for faster rendering and updates
  • Real-time state handling for responsive workflows
  • Improved scalability for future feature growth
  • Modern, user-friendly and responsive interface

Expected Business Impact

The new platform will improve operational speed, reduce system delays, and enable more reliable real-time decision making across inventory, sales, and workforce processes.
